# Project Context

## Business Context

Newland operates across several regions and needs an ERP that can support UAE operations, Iran
operations, Iraq sales and distribution, China sourcing and supplier coordination, and future GCC
expansion.

The platform must support multiple legal entities, companies, branches, warehouses, currencies, and
languages. Persian right-to-left and English left-to-right user experiences are required future
capabilities, but UI implementation is not part of P2.

Newland's core operating model includes imported products, distribution, sales, service, warranty,
project-based accounting, central accounting, project ledgers, central warehouses, branch
warehouses, and project warehouses.

## Required Business Coverage

- Enterprise structure and multi-company operations.
- Product, packaging, carton, unit, weight, CBM, and landed-cost management.
- Supplier sourcing, purchasing, production follow-up, shipment, container, customs, QC, and
  receipt.
- Customer, quotation, sales order, reservation, delivery, invoice, CRM, warranty, and service.
- Double-entry accounting, receivables, payables, banking, checks, cashboxes, and reconciliation.
- Website readiness and future AI integration readiness.

## Non-Assumptions

The following information is not documented and must not be invented:

- Country-specific tax rules.
- Statutory chart-of-accounts mandates.
- Exact company names, legal registration identifiers, and staff names.
- Product catalogs, prices, supplier terms, and customer records.
- Operational transaction volumes.
- Final document numbering formats.
- Final approval limits and financial thresholds.

## OPEN DECISION

- Confirm legal entity list, company list, branch list, and warehouse list.
- Confirm country-specific tax and statutory reporting obligations.
- Confirm fiscal calendars for each legal entity.
- Confirm base currency per legal entity and group reporting currency.
- Confirm final website, AI, payment, banking, logistics, and customs integration priorities.
- Confirm product identifier policy for imported goods, supplier models, Newland model codes, and
  website SKUs.
- Confirm whether project warehouses can hold company-owned stock for multiple projects.
