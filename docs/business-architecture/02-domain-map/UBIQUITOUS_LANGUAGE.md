# Ubiquitous Language

Each term has one preferred meaning inside Newland ERP. Avoid synonyms that blur ownership.

| Term            | Definition                                                                                 | Synonyms to avoid                             | Owning context                                    | Example usage                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enterprise      | The top-level Newland operating group represented in the ERP.                              | Tenant, group company when meaning is unclear | Enterprise Structure                              | Enterprise reporting rolls up approved companies.          |
| Legal Entity    | A legally accountable entity that can own accounting records and statutory obligations.    | Company when statutory identity is meant      | Enterprise Structure                              | A legal entity has its own ledger and base currency.       |
| Company         | An operating company configured under a legal entity for transactions and reporting.       | Branch, tenant                                | Enterprise Structure                              | A purchase order is issued for one company.                |
| Branch          | A business location or unit under a company.                                               | Store, office when scope is unclear           | Enterprise Structure                              | A branch can own sales activity and warehouses.            |
| Warehouse       | A stock-holding facility under a branch or approved project structure.                     | Location, store                               | Enterprise Structure / Inventory                  | Goods are received into a warehouse.                       |
| Location        | A physical bin, shelf, zone, or place inside a warehouse.                                  | Warehouse                                     | Inventory                                         | Put-away moves stock into a location.                      |
| Product         | A master record for an item Newland buys, sells, stores, services, or displays.            | SKU when variant-specific identity is meant   | Product Information Management                    | A product defines brand, category, and packaging.          |
| SKU             | The sellable or stock-keeping identifier used for inventory and sales.                     | Model, product code                           | Product Information Management                    | The SKU is reserved on a sales order.                      |
| Model           | Manufacturer or Newland model reference that may map to one or more SKUs.                  | SKU                                           | Product Information Management                    | Supplier model is captured for sourcing.                   |
| Variant         | A distinguishable version of a product, such as size, color, voltage, or bundle.           | Product duplicate                             | Product Information Management                    | A variant can have separate packaging.                     |
| Unit            | A unit of measure for quantity conversion.                                                 | Piece when not exact                          | Product Information Management                    | Stock is counted in units and cartons.                     |
| Carton          | A packaging unit containing a defined quantity of pieces or units.                         | Box when conversion is unknown                | Product Information Management                    | Carton quantity affects shipment volume.                   |
| Packaging       | The product's packaging structure, dimensions, weight, carton quantity, and handling data. | Unit                                          | Product Information Management                    | Packaging changed triggers review of CBM.                  |
| CBM             | Cubic meter volume calculated from packaging dimensions.                                   | Volume without unit                           | Product Information Management / Import Logistics | CBM supports container planning.                           |
| Lot             | A quantity grouped for traceability by manufacturing or receipt grouping.                  | Batch when legal distinction is needed        | Inventory                                         | Lot readiness is reserved for future traceability.         |
| Batch           | A production or supplier batch identifier.                                                 | Lot when source-controlled                    | Inventory / Quality                               | Batch can be inspected during QC.                          |
| Serial Number   | Unique identifier for one physical unit.                                                   | Barcode                                       | Inventory / Service and Warranty                  | Serial number supports warranty validation.                |
| Available Stock | On-hand stock minus reserved, blocked, QC hold, and other unavailable quantities.          | On-hand                                       | Inventory                                         | Sales checks available stock before reservation.           |
| Reserved Stock  | Stock committed to a demand source but not yet issued.                                     | Allocated, held stock                         | Inventory                                         | Sales order approval can create reservation.               |
| QC Hold         | Stock not available because inspection is pending or failed.                               | Blocked stock                                 | Quality Management / Inventory                    | Goods receipt can place stock on QC hold.                  |
| Blocked Stock   | Stock intentionally unavailable for sale or issue.                                         | QC hold when inspection-specific              | Inventory                                         | Damaged stock is blocked before write-off.                 |
| In Transit      | Stock or goods moving between supplier, port, warehouse, branch, or project locations.     | Available stock                               | Import Logistics / Inventory                      | Container goods are in transit before receipt.             |
| Purchase Order  | Approved commitment to buy from a supplier.                                                | PO draft, proforma                            | Procurement                                       | Purchase order approval starts production follow-up.       |
| Sales Order     | Approved customer commitment to sell and deliver goods or services.                        | Quotation                                     | Sales                                             | Sales order approval requests reservation.                 |
| Goods Receipt   | Posted receipt of goods into warehouse control.                                            | Warehouse entry                               | Inventory                                         | Goods receipt may trigger QC inspection.                   |
| Goods Issue     | Posted removal of stock for delivery, service, transfer, or adjustment.                    | Delivery note                                 | Inventory                                         | Delivery posting creates goods issue.                      |
| Transfer        | Movement of stock between warehouses, zones, locations, branches, or projects.             | Adjustment                                    | Inventory                                         | Transfer requires source and destination.                  |
| Adjustment      | Approved correction movement for count difference, damage, write-off, or correction.       | Direct balance edit                           | Inventory                                         | Adjustment is posted to the inventory ledger.              |
| Journal Entry   | Balanced accounting record with debits and credits.                                        | Voucher when ambiguous                        | General Ledger                                    | Posted journal entry is immutable.                         |
| Account         | Chart-of-accounts node used for financial classification.                                  | GL code only                                  | General Ledger                                    | Account is assigned in a journal line.                     |
| Cost Center     | Management accounting dimension for classifying costs.                                     | Department if department is organizational    | Enterprise Structure / General Ledger             | Expense is tagged to a cost center.                        |
| Project         | A controlled business initiative that may have its own ledger, stock, or cost tracking.    | Job when not approved                         | Enterprise Structure / Project Accounting         | Project warehouse stock is reported by project.            |
| Customer        | Party that buys or receives goods or services.                                             | Client, buyer when role is unclear            | Sales / CRM                                       | Customer has a credit limit.                               |
| Supplier        | Party that provides goods or services to Newland.                                          | Vendor when not standardized                  | Procurement                                       | Supplier quotation is compared before PO.                  |
| Credit Limit    | Approved exposure threshold for a customer.                                                | Payment term                                  | Sales / Accounts Receivable                       | Credit check compares outstanding balance to credit limit. |
| Receivable      | Amount owed by a customer.                                                                 | Collection                                    | Accounts Receivable                               | Posted invoice increases receivable.                       |
| Payable         | Amount owed to a supplier or other party.                                                  | Payment                                       | Accounts Payable                                  | Supplier invoice creates payable.                          |
| Cashbox         | Controlled physical or branch cash holding location.                                       | Petty cash when not exact                     | Treasury / Cashbox                                | Cashbox close requires count.                              |
| Bank Account    | Financial institution account used for payment, receipt, or reconciliation.                | Cash account                                  | Banking / Treasury                                | Bank account statement supports reconciliation.            |
| Check           | Negotiable payment instrument received or issued with lifecycle status.                    | Cheque spelling variant                       | Treasury / Checks                                 | Check maturity affects collection planning.                |
| Shipment        | Inbound or outbound logistics movement grouping goods and transport events.                | Container                                     | Import Logistics                                  | Shipment has ETD and ETA.                                  |
| Container       | Transport unit inside a shipment.                                                          | Shipment                                      | Import Logistics                                  | ContainerLoaded event records loading.                     |
| Landed Cost     | Costs required to bring imported goods to inventory valuation point.                       | Freight only                                  | Import Logistics / Accounting                     | Landed cost allocation is posted after customs data.       |
| Warranty        | Obligation or entitlement for service, repair, or replacement under approved policy.       | Guarantee when not defined                    | Service and Warranty                              | Warranty validation checks sale and product conditions.    |
| Service Ticket  | Trackable after-sales case from request to closure.                                        | Complaint                                     | Service and Warranty                              | Service ticket records diagnosis.                          |
| Approval        | Formal decision allowing a controlled action to proceed.                                   | Review, confirmation                          | Workflow and Approval                             | Approval is required before posting high-risk actions.     |
| Workflow        | Configurable routing process for tasks and approvals.                                      | Business process                              | Workflow and Approval                             | Workflow routes purchase approval.                         |
| Audit Event     | Immutable record of a relevant business or security action.                                | Log when mutable                              | Audit and Compliance                              | Audit event records who posted a journal.                  |
